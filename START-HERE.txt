================================================================
  ELDER TRADES  --  START HERE
================================================================

Everything is in this folder. Do these in order.


  BEFORE YOU TOUCH THE COMPUTER: two things on Alpaca's website
  --------------------------------------------------------------

  Go to your Alpaca PAPER trading dashboard:
  https://app.alpaca.markets/paper/dashboard/overview

  A) RESET the account balance to  1000000

     Why: the bot asks Alpaca how much money you have and sizes
     trades off that answer. If Alpaca says 100,000 it will trade
     like a 100,000 account no matter what any setting says.

  B) TURN ON margin / short selling

     Why: this strategy bets both ways. With shorting off, Alpaca
     silently refuses every short trade and you only get half the
     strategy without being told.


  STEP 1  --  Double-click  1-INSTALL.bat
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  Installs what Python needs. Takes about a minute.
  Wait for it to say DONE, then close the window.


  STEP 2  --  Put your API key in
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  Open  alpaca_keys.txt  in Notepad (right-click > Open with >
  Notepad). You will see:

      ALPACA_API_KEY=PASTE_YOUR_PAPER_KEY_HERE
      ALPACA_SECRET_KEY=PASTE_YOUR_PAPER_SECRET_HERE

  Replace both placeholder bits with your real PAPER key and
  secret. Keep the part before the = sign exactly as it is.
  Save and close.

  Make sure it is your PAPER key, not a live one.


  STEP 3  --  Double-click  2-CHECK.bat
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  This checks about 20 things that could break tomorrow.

  You want to see:     PREFLIGHT PASSED

  "PASSED with N warnings" is fine too. You will always get two
  warnings about "iex" - that just means you are on Alpaca's free
  data. Expected.

  If it says FAILED, scroll up, find the lines marked [FAIL], and
  fix what they tell you. Run it again. Do not go further until
  it passes.


  ================  TOMORROW MORNING  ================

  Market opens 9:30 AM Eastern.


  STEP 4  --  Around 9:00 AM, double-click  2-CHECK.bat  again
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  Things change overnight. Takes 30 seconds.


  STEP 5  --  At 9:30, double-click  3-PRACTICE.bat
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  Practice mode. It watches and thinks but places NOTHING.

  Leave it running 30-60 minutes. You are looking for:
    - data coming in for the symbols
    - [reconcile] lines appearing every 30 seconds
    - no red ERROR lines

  Press Ctrl-C ONCE to stop it.


  STEP 6  --  If that looked healthy, double-click  4-LIVE.bat
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  It will ask you to type YES to confirm.

  Now it places real orders on your PAPER account.
  Fake money. Nothing real is at risk.

  Press Ctrl-C ONCE to stop. Just once, then wait a few seconds.
  Do not close the window - you lose the final report.


  ================  AFTER THE SESSION  ================

  Two folders appear:

     journal\setups_*.csv       trades it TOOK
     journal\rejections_*.csv   trades it SKIPPED, and why

  Open rejections in Excel. Look at the "stage" column.

     mostly "context"        no clear trend today. Normal.
     mostly "location"       trend existed, price never reached
                             a good level. Normal.
     mostly "confirmation"   reached good levels, trigger never
                             fired. If this is EVERY row all day,
                             the trigger is too strict - send it
                             to me and I will loosen it.
     mostly "exits"          setups formed but did not pay enough
     mostly "risk"           safety limits stopped it

  Send me both CSV files and I will tell you what to adjust.


  ================  IMPORTANT  ================

  IT MAY TAKE ZERO TRADES TOMORROW. THAT IS NOT A BUG.

  Three separate conditions have to line up, and the rule is
  "if the move is not obvious, stay away." A quiet day produces
  nothing. That is the strategy working.

  Tomorrow proves the plumbing works - that it connects, reads
  data, sizes correctly, places orders and cleans up after
  itself. It does NOT tell us whether the strategy makes money.
  That needs the backtester, which is the next job.

  Do not change any settings on day one. One day is not enough
  to tune on, and it is easy to accidentally tune to noise.


  ================  IF SOMETHING BREAKS  ================

  "python is not recognized"
      Python is not installed or not on your PATH.
      Get it from python.org and tick "Add Python to PATH".

  Preflight says credentials not found
      alpaca_keys.txt is not saved in THIS folder, or still has
      the placeholder text in it.

  Every short trade rejected
      Shorting is not enabled on the paper account. See (B) above.

  "RISK ENGINE IS HALTED"
      Open state\risk_state.json to see why. Fix it, then delete
      that file to clear the halt.

  A position will not close
      The bot retries automatically. If the log says MANUAL
      INTERVENTION REQUIRED, close it yourself in the Alpaca
      dashboard.


  More detail is in RUNBOOK.md and README.md.
